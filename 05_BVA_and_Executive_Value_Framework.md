# 05 — Executive Business Value Assessment (BVA) Framework

## Industrial Value Pillars & Financial Quantification

### 1. Simulation & Engineering Velocity (Simcenter / NX Integration)
* **Metric:** Reduction in thermal/structural simulation run-times.
* **Baseline:** Average 40 hours per engineering iteration.
* **Impact:** 30% speedup using Maya HTT custom solver acceleration.
* **Financial Value:** $120,000 annual engineering capacity reclaimed per 10-person team.

### 2. Operational Downtime Mitigation (EPIQ-M / Industrial AI)
* **Metric:** Avoidance of critical manufacturing line stoppages.
* **Baseline:** Industry average $30,000/hour downtime expense.
* **Impact:** Early anomaly detection catches failure points 48 hours prior to stoppage.
* **Financial Value:** $360,000+ avoided per enterprise facility annually.
